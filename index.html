BUILT THROUGH GENERATIONS

Our History

1942

Goat Farm Beginnings

What began as a small goat farm laid the foundation for generations of Minnesota agriculture.

1960s

Expansion Into Produce

The farm evolved into large-scale vegetable production, growing radishes, carrots, potatoes, onions, and more.

1980s

Wholesale Growth

Millions of pounds of produce began moving through wholesalers, retailers, processors, and distributors.

TODAY

Heart of the Valley

Today, Ed Fields & Sons continues supplying the Midwest while proudly serving the local community market.
