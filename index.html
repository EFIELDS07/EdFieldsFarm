BUILT THROUGH GENERATIONS

Our History

1930s

Animal Farm Beginnings

Nearly a century ago, Ed Fields purchased his first small piece of land in Andover, Minnesota where he lived alongside his wife, Lorraine, and their eight children. They intended on living an independent farm life, raising livestock and growing a family garden.

1942

Expansion Into Produce

In the year 1942, Ed saw an opportunity to grow beyond a traditional family farm. He expanded production, planting the first seeds of what would become a lasting produce legacy.

1950s

Birth of Heart of the Valley

In the 1950s, Ed's sister, Sister Mary, helped inspire the creation of the Heart of the Valley brand. What began as a way to bring the family's produce directly to local consumers soon became a trusted name throughout Midwest.

1960s

Wholesale Growth

By the 1960s, Ed Fields and Sons was a well-established and successful operation. The farm's reputation for quality and reliability continued to grow as the family expanded both its acreage and operations to meet demand.

TODAY

Growing for the Future

Generations later,Ed's sons and grandsons continue the family farming tradition, carrying forward the values of hard work, quality, and dedication that Ed instilled in the family.

REAL FARM LIFE

If you ate today, thank a farmer

Farming is one of the most demanding ways of life. It requires grit, patience, and determination through every season. Every seed planted is an act of faith, every harvest a reward earned through months of uncertainty and hours of labor. Farmers face long days, unpredictable weather, rising costs, and risks most people never see—yet they continue because feeding others is more than a job; it's a responsibility. While much of the world sleeps, farmers are working to ensure there's food on tomorrow's table.

“The land gives back exactly what you pour into it.” — Chuck Fields

MEET THE FAMILY

Four Generations of Minnesota Farming

What started as a small family operation has grown into one of Minnesota's trusted produce farms. Through changing seasons, equipment, and generations, one thing has remained the same: a commitment to growing quality produce and serving the community.

Long before sunrise, you'll find the Fields family preparing fields, harvesting crops, loading trucks, and helping customers at the farm stand. Farming isn't just what we do—it's who we are.
